URML relative motion on a frameless educational buggy (RFC-0630).
`drive` / `turn` are gated by the educational profile and the
mobility.supports_relative_motion capability; the validator refuses misuse.
robot: classroom_buggy   drive_type: differential

[VALID] educational buggy drives a square (turn 90 + drive 0.5 m, one arc side)

[REJECTED] same program, but the educational profile is not active
   -> capability.relative_motion_requires_educational

[REJECTED] a robot that does not declare supports_relative_motion
   -> capability.relative_motion_unsupported

[REJECTED] a single drive of 5.0 m past the 2.0 m bound
   -> capability.relative_distance_exceeded
