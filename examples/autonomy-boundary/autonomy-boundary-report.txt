URML in the mind of an autonomous robot (Discussion #526).
The mastermind (autonomy) decides; URML validates and executes each physical action.
robot: patrol_bot

# Decisions that stay in the mastermind (autonomy, NOT URML)

  activity hours + battery > 50%   -> may_patrol() = True
  where to wander next              -> autonomy policy
  'is this an unclassified shape?'  -> autonomy (open-set perception)
  store photos tagged to a folder   -> autonomy (filesystem IO)

# Each physical action the mastermind issues becomes a validated URML step

  [autonomy] saw a shape, is_unclassified_shape() = True: stop and photograph it

  [VALID]  turn +30 deg
  [VALID]  drive 0.50 m
  [VALID]  turn -20 deg
  [VALID]  drive 0.50 m
  [VALID]  capture photo (current view)
  [VALID]  turn +45 deg
  [VALID]  drive 0.30 m
  [VALID]  capture photo (current view)
  [VALID]  turn +45 deg
  [VALID]  drive 0.30 m
  [VALID]  capture photo (current view)
  [VALID]  measure distance

# Executed through the mock substrate: 12 steps, success=True

# The boundary, shown by what URML REFUSES

  the mastermind's perception judgment is not a URML step. Expressed as one:
    detect(object: unclassified_shape)  ->  [REJECTED] capability.missing_object_class
    novelty detection is not a declared capability; it stays in autonomy.

Summary: 12 physical actions validated and executed; 1 non-URML decision correctly refused.
The mastermind is free to be as clever as it likes; the robot still only does
what the manifest declares and the envelope allows.
